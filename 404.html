Why a Useful 404 Page Matters on a Legal Site

A weak 404 page wastes user intent, and on a legal-cost site that often means wasting a person who arrived with a real budgeting problem. A strong 404 page should not act like branding fluff. It should act like a map. That is why this page links directly into the highest-intent practice areas, the pricing hub, the calculators, and the research pages instead of just apologizing and stopping.

Search-driven users are often closer to action than site-navigation users. If they hit a dead end, they may not try again. A helpful 404 page reduces that friction and gives them one more clear path to the information they were likely trying to find.
