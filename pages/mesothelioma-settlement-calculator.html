How This Estimate Works

This calculator starts with the modern asbestos-claim reality: strong mesothelioma cases often price from the low seven figures upward, but the correct planning band depends heavily on diagnosis, documented exposure, venue, and whether the claim can pursue both solvent defendants and bankruptcy trusts. A weaker exposure history or a trust-only pathway usually lowers the planning range. A stronger diagnosis, better records, or a veteran-industrial overlap may widen it.

The model is intentionally simple enough to understand. It is not pretending to know the exact products, defendants, or trust percentages in your file. Its purpose is to help you compare scenarios before you talk to firms and before you start treating every ad as a valuation tool.
