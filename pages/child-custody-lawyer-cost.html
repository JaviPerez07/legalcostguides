Child Custody Lawyer Cost

Javi Pérez · Editor LinkedIn Last reviewed: July 2026 · Sources: Clio Legal Trends, BLS OES, U.S. Courts fee schedules
Legal Disclaimer: This content is for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. Always consult a licensed attorney for your specific situation.

On This Page

Child Custody Lawyer Cost depends heavily on whether parents agree, whether the court orders an evaluator, and how many motions the case generates. Each contested hearing adds prep time, and evaluator fees alone often run $5,000–$15,000. Most custody attorneys work from an upfront retainer that they bill against hourly, which means the real driver of your final bill is not the rate itself but how contested the case becomes. A custody matter that settles in mediation and one that goes through a trial with a custody evaluator can start with the same retainer and end in very different places.

The practical budgeting question is not just “What does the lawyer charge?” It is also “What part of the matter is likely to get expensive?” For child custody lawyer cost, that can mean filing steps, records, experts, hearings, negotiations, discovery, or government fees that sit outside the lawyer's own bill. Custody matters can be affordable when parents agree on a parenting plan, but temporary orders, allegations of risk, and evaluations can push costs sharply higher. Custody counsel typically bills hourly against a retainer because the number of motions and hearings is difficult to predict at intake.

How Billing Usually Works

Different billing models exist because different legal problems carry different kinds of uncertainty. Routine, repeatable work is often cheaper to quote as a flat fee. Disputed matters with moving facts often require hourly billing or a replenishing retainer because the lawyer cannot predict the number of filings, calls, edits, or hearings at intake. Custody matters can be affordable when parents agree on a parenting plan, but temporary orders, allegations of risk, and evaluations can push costs sharply higher.
