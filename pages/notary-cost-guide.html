Notary Cost Guide

Javi Pérez · Editor LinkedIn Last reviewed: July 2026 · Sources: Clio Legal Trends, BLS OES, U.S. Courts fee schedules
Legal Disclaimer: This content is for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. Always consult a licensed attorney for your specific situation.

On This Page

Notary Cost Guide is set by state-by-state fee caps, often capped per signature ($1–$15 in most states). Mobile notaries add travel, online RON adds platform fees, and bank notaries are sometimes free for customers. Flat per-signature pricing dominates here rather than hourly billing, so what you pay usually comes down to where and how the notarization happens. The state's fee cap sets the floor, and convenience charges — a notary who drives to you, an after-hours appointment, an online session — account for most of the difference between one quote and the next.

The practical budgeting question is not just “What does the lawyer charge?” It is also “What part of the matter is likely to get expensive?” For notary cost guide, that can mean filing steps, records, experts, hearings, negotiations, discovery, or government fees that sit outside the lawyer's own bill. Basic notarizations are usually inexpensive, but mobile notary travel fees, loan-signing services, and after-hours appointments raise the bill. Consumers often confuse notary pricing with legal-document review. A notarization is not a substitute for advice about what the document means.

Do-it-yourself option

A notary certifies signatures — it does not draft the document

How Billing Usually Works

Different billing models exist because different legal problems carry different kinds of uncertainty. Routine, repeatable work is often cheaper to quote as a flat fee. Disputed matters with moving facts often require hourly billing or a replenishing retainer because the lawyer cannot predict the number of filings, calls, edits, or hearings at intake. Basic notarizations are usually inexpensive, but mobile notary travel fees, loan-signing services, and after-hours appointments raise the bill.
